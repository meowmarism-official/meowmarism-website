What is planned

Plans can change. This is the direction, not a promise of dates or features.

Docker isolationEvery instance in its own container, with its own network and volumes, and an image per Minecraft version and loader.
Real resource limitsHard CPU and RAM limits per instance through container cgroups, not just a JVM heap setting.
Several servers, one panelManage instances across multiple physical hosts from one place, with a proxy such as Velocity for one network.
The same permission modelThe permission model, look and feel of LITE, scaled to teams and many customers.

Built on the same foundations

LITE and PROFESSIONAL share design, brand and the permission model through meowmarism core. Everything you learn in LITE carries over.

LITE keeps goingLITE stays free and open source. PROFESSIONAL does not replace it.
