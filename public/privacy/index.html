Server logs

When you open a page, the web server keeps a standard access log entry: IP address, timestamp, the requested path, status code, user-agent, and referrer. This is needed to run and secure the site (spotting abuse, fixing outages). The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR, our legitimate interest in a secure and reliable website. Access logs are deleted automatically after 14 days. They are not used for tracking and not merged with other data.
