What is meowmarism?

A control panel for Minecraft servers that runs on your own hardware. Create servers, watch the live console and statistics, install mods, take backups, schedule restarts and share access, all from your browser.

  • Live consoleServer output and commands in real time.
  • StatisticsCPU, memory, disk and network, with a history of seven days.
  • Mods and backupsInstall from Modrinth, back up and restore worlds.
  • Access for othersAccounts with exactly the permissions you give.
