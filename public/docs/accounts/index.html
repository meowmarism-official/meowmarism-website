Accounts and permissions

People get exactly the permissions you choose, per instance, and the panel enforces them twice.

The owner

The owner is created by the installer and always has full access. The owner account cannot be edited or deleted by anyone else.

Panel-wide permissions are users (manage accounts and panel settings), create (create instances) and update (update the panel).

Default and per instance

Each account has a default set for all instances and can be overridden for individual instances. Someone with no permission on an instance does not see it in the list.

Rules

  • An account with manage users can only be changed by the owner.
  • An administrator cannot hand out the right to manage users.
  • The controller checks every request, and each worker checks it again, so a change on one side cannot bypass the other.

Login protection

Failed logins are throttled per IP address and username: after three failures the wait grows (5 seconds, 10, 20 and so on, up to 15 minutes), and 20 failures from one IP in ten minutes lock that IP for 15 minutes. While locked, even the right password is refused until the time is up. Sessions survive panel restarts and updates.
