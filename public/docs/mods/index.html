Mods and plugins

Search Modrinth, install with required dependencies and keep everything up to date, without touching SSH.

The Mods page has three tabs: Installed, Browse Modrinth and Updates. Paper and Purpur servers use the plugins folder, all other types use mods. Vanilla servers have neither.

Installed

Lists every jar in the folder and lets you disable or enable it. Disabled files move to disabled_mods and are not loaded. Changes take effect after a server restart.

Browse Modrinth

The search only shows projects that are compatible with the instance's Minecraft version and loader. Every result is a card with the icon, downloads, followers, categories and when it was last updated. Sort by relevance, downloads, followers, newest or recently updated.

Install on a card takes the newest release and adds required dependencies automatically. Click a card to open the project: its description, gallery, the compatible versions (each with its own Install), the license, the environment (client and server), the team and links to the source, issues and Modrinth.

Few downloads

Projects with fewer than 50,000 downloads carry a warning on the card and in the detail view. Fewer people have tried them, so read the description and look at the source code before you install one.

Other project types

Above the results you can switch between mods (or plugins), datapacks, resource packs, shaders and modpacks.

  • Datapacks are installed into the datapacks folder of the world and load after a restart or /reload. They also work on vanilla servers.
  • Resource packs, shaders and modpacks are used by players, not by the server. You can browse them here and open them on Modrinth, but they cannot be installed on the server.

Images in the search and the detail view are fetched through the panel. Your browser never contacts Modrinth.

Updates

Installed files are identified by their hash, so mods you added by hand are found too. The tab shows:

  • mods that have a newer release, with Update and Update all;
  • mods that have no compatible version for this server;
  • files that are not on Modrinth and therefore not checked.

Replaced files move to mods-old (or plugins-old) instead of being deleted, so you can go back.

Safety

Downloads are only accepted from Modrinth's CDN and are checked against their SHA-512 and size. A file that fails the check is discarded.

Permissions

Installing and updating needs the mods permission on the instance.
