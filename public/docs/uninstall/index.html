Uninstall

Removing the panel and removing your servers are two separate, explicitly confirmed steps.

Run the installer again, press M for more options, then R to remove:

curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/meowmarism-official/meowmarism-lite/master/install.sh | bash
  1. Removing the panel

    It lists what will happen (the service is stopped and deleted, the panel files are removed, running Minecraft servers are stopped) and your instances. You must type remove to continue.

  2. Removing your data (optional)

    Your instances, backups and accounts are only deleted if you additionally type delete everything. Anything else keeps them.

Careful

Deleting data removes worlds, mods, configurations, backups and accounts permanently.

PROFESSIONAL

The same menu applies: run the install command, press M, then R. Your Minecraft containers and the data in ~/meowmarism-pro are kept unless you type the second confirmation to delete everything.
